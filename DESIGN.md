# Bunker Wallet design system

## Direction

A secure operations runbook: one next decision at a time, with the clarity of overhead wayfinding and the restraint of a hardware verification sheet. It refuses a dashboard of market data.

## Palette

Graphite `#08100e`, deep surface `#0e1916`, raised state `#13221e`, rule `#253c35`, paper text `#eef8f4`, secondary `#99afa7`, and mint signal `#5fe0b4`. Light mode reverses to cool paper while preserving semantic contrast. No gradients.

## Typography and composition

Native UI sans avoids third-party requests. Large headings are tightly tracked but stay below 6rem. Addresses and envelope data use native monospace. A 180px desktop command rail becomes a four-action mobile dock. Status is conveyed through bands, rules, plain language, and one restrained accent—not decorative cards.

## Components and states

Buttons are squared with small radii, clear borders, visible focus, disabled opacity, and direct verbs. Flow stages use numbered status bands. Warning, loading/locked, validation error, success, and disabled states remain in normal document flow. Motion is limited to theme transition and disabled for reduced-motion users.
